Understanding China Eastern Baggage Policy

China Eastern Airlines follows a structured baggage policy that varies depending on your travel class, route, and fare type. The airline uses both the weight concept and piece concept for baggage allowance.

Carry-On Baggage Allowance

Cabin Baggage Rules

  • Economy Class: 1 bag, max 8 kg
  • Business & First Class: 2 bags, max 8 kg each

Size Restrictions

Maximum dimensions: 55 cm x 40 cm x 20 cm

Important Tips

  • Ensure your bag fits overhead
  • Keep valuables in carry-on
  • Avoid restricted items

Checked Baggage Allowance

Domestic Flights

  • Economy: 20 kg
  • Business: 30 kg
  • First: 40 kg

International Flights

  • Economy: 1–2 pieces (23 kg each)
  • Business: 2 pieces (32 kg each)
  • First: 3 pieces (32 kg each)

Size Limitations

Total dimensions must not exceed 158 cm.

Excess Baggage Fees

Fees apply if weight, size, or number of bags exceed limits. Pre-booking extra baggage online is usually cheaper than airport charges.

Special Baggage Rules

Sports Equipment

Includes golf clubs, skis, surfboards.

Musical Instruments

Small instruments allowed in cabin; large ones may require extra seat.

Fragile Items

Use proper packaging and inform airline staff.

Baggage for Infants and Children

  • Infants: 1 checked bag + stroller
  • Children: Same allowance as adults

Restricted and Prohibited Items

  • Explosives, flammable materials
  • Toxic substances
  • Weapons

Lost, Delayed, or Damaged Baggage

Report immediately at the airport and file a PIR. Compensation depends on airline policies and international rules.

Tips to Avoid Baggage Issues

  • Check allowance before packing
  • Weigh bags at home
  • Label your luggage

How to Save on Baggage Fees

  • Book the right fare
  • Join frequent flyer programs
  • Prepay baggage online

Knowing baggage rules ensures faster check-in, stress-free boarding, and better travel planning.
